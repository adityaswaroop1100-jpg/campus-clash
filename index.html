MISSION DIRECTIVE

Welcome to AARUUSH CLASH: CAMPUS CLASH — the premier SRMIST nationwide techno-management combat simulation.

CORE OBJECTIVE:

Navigate all 17 Portals across SRM Kattankulathur Campus within the 180-second clock window. Neutralize rival department champions and claim the ultimate 10 CGPA victory.

KEY PROTOCOLS

  • Fighter Archetypes: Choose from 10 distinct campus legends including Topper, Backbencher, Hosteler, Senior, Placement Warrior, and Cypher.
  • Campus Arenas: Battle across authentic SRM landmarks — Tech Park Steps, UB Clock Tower, Dr. T.P. Ganesan Auditorium, and the Grand Arch Gate.
  • Deterministic Combat: Frame-accurate hitboxes, cancelable light/heavy attacks, directional blocking, and devastating cinematic Ultimates.
  • Live Leaderboard: All clearance records and battle telemetry sync directly with the festival Supabase cloud database.
